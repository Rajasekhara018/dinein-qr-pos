Account recovery

Locked out? We'll get you back in.

Staff accounts are set up and reset by your restaurant's owner or a manager, so a password reset always starts with them.

Forgot your password?

There is no self-service reset in the admin panel. Ask your restaurant's owner or a manager to sign in and set a new temporary password for your account from Staff & devices — you'll be asked to choose your own password the next time you sign in.
